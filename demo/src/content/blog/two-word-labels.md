---
title: Labels in two words or fewer
date: '2026-07-22'
summary: Small uppercase labels are quick to scan and slow to read. Keeping them to one or two words keeps them in the first group.
tags: [Type, Writing]
---

Uppercase text has no ascenders or descenders to give each word its shape, so we read it letter by letter. For a word or two that costs nothing. For a sentence it costs a lot.

## Where the kit uses caps

- Tab names: Menu, Contact, Features.
- Button text: Send, Read post.
- Tiny labels over values: Published, Reading, Topics.

Each one is a word or two, and each one is a name for something, not a description of it.

## When a label wants to be longer

Rewrite it shorter, or set it in sentence case. "Filter by category" reads better as it is than as FILTER BY CATEGORY, and the kit's `.prose-sentence` class exists for exactly that case.
