Movement draws the eye harder than color does, so the kit spends it as carefully as the accent.

## What moves

1. The panel and drawer slide down, in about half a second.
2. Accordions open and close.
3. A title too long for its line scrolls, three rounds at most, then rests.
4. A row you jump to flashes once.

## What doesn't

Images never pan or zoom. Nothing loops forever. Hover changes swap color in 150 milliseconds and don't move anything.

## Reduce Motion

With the system's Reduce Motion setting on, the slides, fades and loops switch off entirely. The page works the same; it just arrives without moving.
