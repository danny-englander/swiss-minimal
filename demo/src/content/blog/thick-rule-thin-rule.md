---
title: Thick rule, thin rule
date: '2026-08-14'
summary: Two line weights do all the structural work on a page. One starts a section; the other separates the rows inside it.
tags: [Layout]
---

A page in this kit has two kinds of line, and only two.

## The thick rule

A 2px line in the ink color starts every section. It sits above the heading, not below it, so the eye meets the line first and reads it as "something new begins here". The top bar ends with one, the footer starts with one, and so does every list.

## The thin rule

A 1px line in a lighter tone separates rows inside a section. It is quiet on purpose. It tells you where one item stops, and nothing more.

## Why only two

A third weight would need a rule of its own for when to use it. Two weights need only one: thick between sections, thin between rows. Anyone adding a page can follow that without asking.

> A line should say where something starts or stops. If it says anything else, it is decoration.
